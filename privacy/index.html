Privacy Policy

Don't Call Your Ex · Effective date: October 3, 2026

Don't Call Your Ex ("the app") is built to work entirely on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, store or share any personal data. The only data that leaves your device is technical purchase diagnostics, sent directly to Google by the Google Play billing library (see Purchases).

Contacts

With your permission, the app reads and changes the contacts you choose to protect:

  • It replaces their phone numbers with the redirect you selected and adds an "Open this instead" link to the contact.
  • It keeps a copy of the original numbers so it can restore them. This copy is stored on your device: in the app's private storage and in a hidden field of the protected contact, so the numbers can be restored even if the app is reinstalled.
  • It stores the trial status (for example, how many uses are left) in the same hidden field, so reinstalling the app does not reset it.

If you pick your trusted person from your contacts, the app reads only the name and number you select. This does not need the contacts permission.

The app never uploads your contacts anywhere. If your contacts are synced with an account (for example Google or iCloud), that account provider syncs the changed numbers and the link the same way it syncs any other edit you make. Turning protection off restores the original numbers and removes the link.

Media files

If you choose a song, video or image as your redirect, the app copies it into its private storage on your device so it is always available. It is never uploaded.

Messages

"Text a friend instead" opens your messaging app with your text addressed to a person you trust. The app never sends anything by itself.

Notifications

The app shows local notifications, for example before your trial protection ends. They are created on your device; no push service is used.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. The app only receives confirmation of what you bought. We never see your payment details. On Android, the Google Play billing library built into the app also sends technical diagnostic data about the purchase process directly to Google. We do not receive this data. This is required for purchases to work and is encrypted in transit. Their handling is covered by Google's and Apple's privacy policies.

No tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Data retention and your rights

Apart from the purchase diagnostics described above, all data the app uses stays on your device. The app's own storage is removed when you uninstall the app. The hidden field in a protected contact stays in that contact after uninstalling: it holds the trial status and, if protection was still on, the original numbers, so they can be restored after reinstalling. It is removed when you delete the contact. Because we do not collect or store any data about you, we have nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You stay in full control of your data on your device.

Deleting your data

Turn protection off for all contacts, then uninstall the app. This restores the original numbers and removes the app's storage from your device. The trial status stays in the hidden field of the contacts that were protected until you delete those contacts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Apart from the purchase diagnostics described above, it does not collect any data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
