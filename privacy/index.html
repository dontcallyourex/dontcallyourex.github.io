Privacy Policy

Don't Call Your Ex · Effective date: November 16, 2026

Don't Call Your Ex ("the app") is built to work entirely on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, store or share any personal data. Nothing the app uses leaves your device: the app does not connect to the internet.

Contacts

With your permission, the app reads and changes the contacts you choose to protect:

  • It replaces their phone numbers with your redirect number and adds an "Open this instead" link to the contact.
  • It keeps a copy of the original numbers so it can restore them. This copy is stored on your device: in the app's private storage and in a backup copy, so the numbers can be restored even if the app is reinstalled. On Android the backup copy is a hidden field of the protected contact. On iPhone and iPad it is kept in the device's Keychain, set to stay on this device only: it is never synced to iCloud.

The backup copy is removed when you turn protection off. If you uninstall the app while protection is on, it stays on your device so the numbers can be restored after reinstalling. On Android, deleting the contact also removes it.

If you pick your trusted person or your redirect number from your contacts, the app reads only the name and number you select. This does not need the contacts permission.

The app never uploads your contacts anywhere. If your contacts are synced with an account (for example Google or iCloud), that account provider syncs the changed numbers and the link the same way it syncs any other edit you make. Turning protection off restores the original numbers and removes the link.

If you tap "Export original numbers", the app opens your phone's share menu with the names and original numbers of your protected contacts as text. You choose where to send or save it; the app sends nothing by itself.

Calls

Screening incoming calls is optional and off by default.

On Android, if you choose to silence or block calls from protected contacts, your phone asks you to make the app your call screening app. The app then compares the number of each incoming call with the original numbers of your protected contacts, only on your device. It does not read your call history and never sends any call information anywhere.

If you turn on "Show when your ex tries to call you", the app records the time of each screened call and which protected contact it came from, without the number, on your device only, for 30 days. Turning it off deletes these records. This option is on Android only.

On iPhone, if you choose to block calls from protected contacts, you turn the app on in the phone's call blocking settings. The app then gives iOS the list of the original numbers of your protected contacts, and iOS blocks calls from them by itself. The list stays on your device, in the Keychain, and is emptied when you stop blocking. The app never sees your calls or your call history.

Media files

If you choose a song, video or image as your redirect, the app copies it into its private storage on your device so it is always available. It is never uploaded.

Opening another app

If you choose to open a dating app instead of your song, video or image, the app only checks whether that app is installed on your phone and opens it. It does not see your other installed apps, passes nothing to the app it opens and does not see what you do there.

If you tap "Find a support line near me", the app opens your browser with a web search for support lines; the search contains only fixed text, never your contacts or numbers. The search engine's own privacy policy applies to that search.

Messages and calls

"Text a friend instead" opens your messaging app with your text addressed to a person you trust. The app never sends anything by itself.

If you choose "Call someone you trust", the app opens your phone app with that person's number typed in. You start the call yourself; the app does not make or see the call.

Notifications

The app shows local notifications, for example when the original numbers are back after you turned protection off (on iPhone and iPad: when you can open the app to bring them back). They are created on your device; no push service is used.

Purchases

The app is paid once, when you download it from Google Play or the App Store. The payment is handled by the store under its own privacy policy. The app has no in-app purchases and receives no payment information.

No tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Data retention and your rights

All data the app uses stays on your device. The app's own storage is removed when you uninstall the app. If protection was still on, the backup copy (the hidden field in a protected contact on Android, the Keychain on iPhone and iPad) keeps the original numbers after uninstalling, so they can be restored after reinstalling. It is removed when you turn protection off. Because we do not collect or store any data about you, we have nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You stay in full control of your data on your device.

Deleting your data

Turn protection off for all contacts. After 24 hours, when the original numbers are back and the backup copies are removed (on iPhone and iPad: open the app once after the 24 hours), uninstall the app. This removes the app's storage from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. It does not collect any data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
